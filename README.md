## Current Research Direction

My primary research project is **AELIA**, where I am investigating how an artificial entity can maintain a continuous cognitive existence rather than behaving as a sequence of isolated prompt-response interactions.

The central idea is that an intelligent system should not be defined solely by the language model producing its outputs. Instead, its behavior can emerge from a persistent architecture containing **memory**, **beliefs**, **world and self models**, **internal states**, **motivations**, **relationships**, and accumulated experience.

A simplified view of this process is:

**Perception → Memory & Belief → Appraisal → Internal State → Goals → Deliberation → Action → Experience & Learning**

I am particularly interested in how these components can produce **identity continuity**, **long-term behavioral adaptation**, **social behavior**, and increasingly self-directed activity.

In parallel, I develop [**Opus Client**](https://github.com/project-opusmc/opus), using Minecraft as a practical environment for experimenting with **runtime architecture**, **performance optimization**, **rendering**, **input and networking systems**, and deeply integrated client software.

My long-term goal is to explore artificial systems that are not merely **task solvers**, but persistent and structured entities whose behavior evolves through **memory, cognition, internal state, experience, and interaction with their environment**.
